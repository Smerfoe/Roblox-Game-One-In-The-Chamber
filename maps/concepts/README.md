These are the six STANDARD-map names from the original game roadmap, now explored visually. This is a concept review package, not six built maps or proof of balance. Existing 1v1 models and game systems are unchanged. Start with one approved blockout after the intended team mode works.

## Shared gameplay requirements

Keep the existing first-to-three/best-of-five framework, three-minute rounds, one life, one server-authorized bullet, pistol/knife damage rules and no friendly fire. New scenery does not authorize gameplay changes or new hazards. Team-mode readiness is a separate task from map art.

- Two opposing protected spawn zones, with capacity for the intended team size. Two usable exits per spawn and no direct spawn-to-spawn ray. Multiple spawn points must have character clearance and not share the same position. Face toward arena exits.
- Three connected broad route families and more than one cross-link. No unintentional dead ends, single mandatory choke, inaccessible corner or one dominant cover piece.
- Short/medium encounters and purposeful choices between covered rotations and exposed crossings. One-bullet gameplay needs routes that allow closing for melee without forcing blind tunnel fights every round.
- Equal opportunity for both teams: compare travel time to first contested cover, early sightlines and flank timing. Visual asymmetry is allowed only with tested collision/opportunity balance. Do not claim the concept is balanced based on an image.
- Single playable elevation for this first pass. Keep stairs, ladders, rooftop perches, moving vehicles and damage hazards out. Invisible anti-climb/fall collision is acceptable only when deliberate, documented and playtested for projectile behavior.
- Simple collision and readable targets. No excessive props, dense fog, deep shadows or surface effects that hide opponents. Keep fixed coordinates, named modules and clean edges; no randomized placement.

## Distinct arena directions

### Freight Terminal

A compact distribution yard rather than another container maze. Offset walk-through loading office blocks the main sightline; outer dock/service routes link behind purposeful cargo cover. Trucks/loading equipment are mostly outside bounds. No accessible vehicle roofs. Overcast neutral lighting, concrete and restrained steel colors. Validate the office entrances and dock corners against camping.

### Foundry

Decommissioned metalworks with one heavy opaque casting machine as the landmark. Side service aisles and cross-aisles create two-player trading opportunities without long empty factory lanes. Brick, iron and practical warm light with daylight fill. No molten metal, fire, pits or accessible catwalks. Machinery foundations must not form unintended step ladders.

### Canal Works

A dry treatment-facility courtyard with small utility buildings and grounded pump cover. Drainage is shallow/flush and traversable, not a mandatory sunken choke. High pipes follow boundaries. Real water tanks may remain beyond the play area as scenery. Test cross-links and side routes for three-person rotations rather than a single center-door fight.

### Metro Exchange

One concourse level. Kiosk/columns break sightlines; opposing ticket-control rooms protect spawns. Platform/train scenery is behind a clear collision boundary and does not create rail pits or moving hazards. Keep lighting readable and tile reflections restrained. Test column peeks for unequal exposed-body advantage and avoid countless tiny cover poles.

### Skyline

All gameplay is on the SAME roof elevation. Mechanical penthouses and HVAC banks make three connected service routes. High continuous parapet/mesh prevents falls; no ledges, parkour or reachable secondary roofs. Space supports four-player spacing and trades. Confirm that walking to the perimeter never exposes unfair sky silhouettes on one side or permits outside-boundary shots.

### Alpine Station

LATEST USER DIRECTION: very basic, classic Roblox STUDDED geometry only. Use a white studded baseplate to suggest snow, light-gray simple perimeter walls, one small roofless central block structure with offset door gaps, and four purposefully placed basic cover blocks. Blue/red flat floor tiles identify protected opposing spawn pockets. Keep three connected ground routes and clear four-player spawn capacity. No detailed research props, textured snow, custom meshes, mountain scenery, satellite equipment, trees, realistic weathering or cinematic lighting. Use standard simple materials and native studded surfaces; the image is an art reference, not an instruction to create hundreds of separate stud Parts. Geometry stays anchored and grid-aligned. Retain single-floor gameplay and check target visibility against pale surfaces. The initial footprint remains a proposal to test, not an approved construction dimension. The realistic Alpine panel in Set B is superseded; do not build that version.

## Suggested build order

1. Approve one concept per first target mode; do not build all six at once.
2. Make a dimensioned overhead plan with widths, spawn points, boundary heights and first-contact sightlines.
3. Graybox only the required map. Inspect actual character scale and collision in Studio.
4. Run the intended mode with enough clients: four for 2v2, six for 3v3, eight for 4v4. Two clients do not establish team-map readiness.
5. Verify spawn protection, opening timing, trading/flank routes, friendly-fire rejection, one-ammo award, spectating targets, round reset and match return. Fix the blockout before decorating.
6. Add approved material/prop pass and rerun readability, jump/slide exploits and performance checks.
7. Integrate using the existing map/spawn contract only after test evidence. Preserve a recoverable copy of the working 1v1 arena. Do not publish.

No build, team-mode integration or Studio playtests were performed for these six concepts.
